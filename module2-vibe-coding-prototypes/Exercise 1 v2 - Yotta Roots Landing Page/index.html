929.1 · 02 — Our reasoning

Built for research, not engagement

The big platforms are built to keep you clicking and to grow their database. We built Yotta Roots for careful, sourced research.

i.

Family trees, not a database of humanity

The LDS project and major sites like Ancestry share a goal: one massive genealogical record of everyone who ever lived. We think that's the wrong goal. On Yotta Roots, data moves into and out of an instance only for the families represented in the trees hosted there. Your research stays tied to the people you're researching, not merged into someone else's dataset.

ii.

Less noise. Much less.

Tens of thousands of unreviewed "hints" based on vaguely similar names aren't help, they're homework. Yotta Roots only suggests connections from researchers you've chosen to follow, and every suggestion comes with its sources and a confidence score, so you can triage it in seconds.

Typical site31,482 hints
Yotta Roots7 sourced leads
iii.

You control your network

Connect your instance only to researchers you trust: a cousin in Ohio, a professional colleague, a county historical society. Federation is opt-in for each connection, and you can revoke it any time.

iv.

Confidence scores on everything

Every fact, event, and relationship carries a weighted confidence score derived from its evidence. Weak links look weak in the tree. Strong ones earn their place. When you share, collaborators see your reasoning as well as your conclusions.

Community

A small social network for every family

  • Every instance is a micro-social network hub, connected to researchers with an interest in the same family: cousins, colleagues, and local societies.
  • The built-in social platform doubles as a family Mastodon server, fully integrated with the Fediverse. Follow and be followed from any compatible account.
  • Share findings, ask for help with a brick wall, or announce a reunion, all next to the tree it's about.

Versioned trees

Branch the tree when the evidence is unclear

  • Create experimental branches when the data gets fuzzy. Two baptisms, one name? Model both, side by side, and let the evidence decide.
  • Roll back changes when you realize you've strayed down the wrong path. Every edit is recorded with who made it, when, and why.
  • Merge a proven hypothesis into your main tree, or archive it with notes so nobody repeats the dead end.

Data ownership & privacy

Your family is not a product

  • All data is exportable in a variety of standard and archival formats: GEDCOM 5.5.1 & 7, GEDCOM-X, CSV, and a self-describing archival bundle with sources, media, and full version history.
  • Yotta Roots will never share data or do business with data brokers. Not for "research partnerships", not if we're acquired, not ever. It's in our terms, in plain English.
  • Living persons are private by default. Fine-grained sharing per tree, per branch, and per person.

Empowering · free & open source

Infrastructure you can inspect, extend, and run yourself

  • Built on the FadoDevStack, a Docker Swarm-based open source self-hosting infrastructure platform. It includes service orchestration, TLS, automated backups, and monitoring.
  • Every service is a container: tree engine, Fediverse server, database, media store, and search. Swap, scale, or replace any of them.
  • A documented plugin API for custom record types, importers, reports, and visualizations. Your scripts, your rules.
