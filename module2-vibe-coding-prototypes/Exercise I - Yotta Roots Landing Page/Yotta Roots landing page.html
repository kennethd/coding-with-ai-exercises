Built for people who cite their sources

Four commitments that shape every part of Yotta Roots.

Own your data

Your tree lives on your server or your isolated instance, in open formats. Share a branch, a person or nothing at all, per collaborator, and revoke it any time. Full export is always free.

Community, federated

Follow and message researchers on other independent servers, the way email and Mastodon work. Share findings and questions without handing your tree to a central platform.

Versioned

Every edit is recorded. Branch to test a hypothesis (was she a Halloran or a Cahill?), then compare, merge or discard. The original record is never destroyed.

Empowering

Free and open source from database to interface. Write importers, citation templates and reports as plugins, or read every line of code that touches your research.

Who we are

Founder

Three decades of building for the web

Yotta Roots was started by an engineer with 30 years of web and data engineering behind them and 25 years working with Free Software and Data Privacy groups. The aim: a research tool that treats genealogy like the library science it is, with provenance, citations and respect for your records.

Community

Researchers who build their own tools

Our contributors are tech-minded amateur genealogists who want solutions that let people own their data and services, not another platform holding your work hostage for a fee. Bring your research problems, your code, or both.
